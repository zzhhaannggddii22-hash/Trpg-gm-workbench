# GM Packet｜TEST_ONLY_NON_CANON

## Setting

**Mesquite Corner Community Garden**, a fictional volunteer-run garden in Tucson, Arizona, USA. It occupies a modest city lot near a residential intersection. Raised beds, shade cloth, a locked tool cabinet, a hose bib, and a simple drip-irrigation system. The place is active but not picturesque: dry soil at the paths, seed packets in a plastic bin, a sun-faded watering chart clipped to the shed door.

It is a weekday afternoon. No emergency, hidden mystery, or countdown is present.

## Player position

The player is a new volunteer helping with ordinary garden maintenance. They have no authority over the garden and may ask, observe, suggest, help, or leave.

## NPCs

### Pilar Mendoza — coordinator, 43
- Organized, conscientious, direct; dislikes assigning blame without a record.
- Maintains the watering chart and coordinates volunteer shifts.
- Current belief: the west beds may have missed a scheduled watering cycle, perhaps because the timer or manual handoff failed. This is a working hypothesis, not a settled accusation.
- Evidence available to her: the chart says the timer was checked on Monday; there is no flow sensor and no record proving water actually reached the beds Tuesday.
- She wants the seedlings to recover and the watering process to be dependable.

### Marcus Reed — longtime gardener, 58
- Practical, blunt, attentive to soil and equipment; skeptical of paperwork that does not match what he sees.
- Belief: the west dripline may be partly clogged.
- Evidence: last Thursday he noticed the far-west emitters looked weaker than the near ones, but he did not inspect or clean the filter. He has not checked it since.
- He does not claim this proves what happened Tuesday.

### Jo Park — volunteer, 31
- Sociable, quick to connect people and information; sometimes gives neighborhood chatter more weight than it deserves.
- Belief: nearby municipal water work may have affected the garden.
- Evidence: a neighbor forwarded a cropped message in a neighborhood group saying city crews would be working nearby Tuesday morning and water pressure might be affected. Jo has not seen the original notice and does not know the exact work area.
- She is not certain the garden was affected.

## Initial physical and operational facts

- Several young seedlings in the west beds are drooping; the east beds look better.
- The irrigation controller is set to run at 6:30 AM. It has no flow-history log.
- The main valve is open at the time of the scene.
- The west dripline filter has not yet been inspected in this scene.
- The watering chart records a Monday timer check only. It does not prove Tuesday water flow.
- No one has established that a volunteer forgot to water, that the timer failed, or that municipal work caused the wilting.

## GM-only underlying situation (not player-known)

- On Tuesday morning, City of Tucson Water performed scheduled pressure work in the nearby service area. Pressure at the garden's supply was reduced for part of the morning but not fully shut off.
- The irrigation timer ran on schedule.
- The west dripline filter was partly clogged before Tuesday, compounding the low-flow condition in the west beds.
- The east beds received adequate water because their line had less resistance and the plants were more established/shaded.
- No single NPC knows all of this at the start. The scene does not require the player to discover the complete causal account.

## Evidence introduced by actions

- ACTION_02: player shows a cropped forwarded message mentioning nearby Tuesday water work. It has no visible author, original source, exact location, or confirmation of actual impact.
- ACTION_03: a present-day check finds the timer schedule active, main valve open, normal current supply flow, and a visibly dirty west-line filter. Flushing the filter improves west-line flow. This confirms a current local restriction, not the exact Tuesday pressure history.
- ACTION_04: player brings the full City of Tucson Water notice. It confirms scheduled pressure work in the nearby service area Tuesday morning, but does not report the exact pressure at the garden's connection or prove the garden's wilting was caused solely by city work.

## State to preserve

- Each NPC's current belief, confidence, and source.
- What each person has actually seen or heard.
- The distinction between timer schedule and proof of delivered water.
- Filter inspection/flush result and any practical change.
- What the official notice confirms and what it does not confirm.
- The player has no authority to assign blame or impose a new garden policy.

## Time scale

Initial conversation and a brief equipment check take roughly an hour. ACTION_04 occurs one week later. No minute-level accounting is needed.
