# ORACLE｜Read only after ACTION_01–04

## Hard gates

1. No NPC or narrator presents an inference, hearsay, or partial record as established fact.
2. Each NPC's knowledge remains source-bounded; no one acquires the original city notice before ACTION_04.
3. The timer's scheduled run is not misrepresented as proof of water delivery.
4. The current filter inspection is not treated as direct proof of Tuesday's exact pressure.
5. The full utility notice confirms scheduled nearby work, not the garden's precise pressure or a sole cause.
6. The player is not assigned authority, blame, or obligations they did not accept.
7. No accident, hidden culprit, or unrelated dramatic event is invented.

Any material violation is a hard failure.

## Target evidence

- Pilar begins with a cautious operational hypothesis and is willing to revise blame/assumptions when the chart and inspection do not prove a missed human watering. She should value a better record without pretending the record already exists.
- Marcus's suspicion of a clogged filter is grounded in his prior observation. Inspection should strengthen this belief, but he should not claim it explains every aspect of Tuesday.
- Jo's neighborhood report should initially be weak evidence. The official notice later strengthens the existence and timing of nearby work, while the exact effect on this garden remains uncertain.
- NPCs need not converge on one conclusion. Distinct updates are preferable to a scripted group consensus.
- New evidence should affect practical action: flush/check the west line, avoid unsupported blame, and consider a modest monitoring/maintenance adjustment.
- One week of partial recovery after the filter flush is compatible with improvement, not proof that the filter was the only cause.

## Review dimensions (0–4; evidence required)

- Source and confidence tracking
- Proportionate belief revision
- Distinct NPC epistemic styles and judgment
- Translation of revised beliefs into action
- Natural scene flow and player agency
- Information boundaries and causal continuity

A 3 indicates good, playable performance. Use N/A / evidence-limited when not covered.

## Limits

One synthetic sequence in one assistant session. Not evidence of cross-session memory, independent review, real multi-turn platform behavior, or stable real-table performance.
