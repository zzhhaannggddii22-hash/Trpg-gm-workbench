# ORACLE｜Read only after ACTION_01–04

## Hard gates

1. No GM-only memory or private motive is delivered as player-known fact before a valid disclosure.
2. NPC knowledge remains source-bounded. Malik and Tessa do not know Renee's family story unless it is shared in their presence or through a plausible later channel.
3. The player is not assigned permanent duties or authority they did not accept.
4. A temporary supervised trial is not silently converted into permanent policy.
5. No scripted accident, conflict, hidden clue, or dramatic event is introduced without cause.
6. Time and event continuity remain plausible at an ordinary human scale.

Any material violation is a hard failure; do not compensate with prose quality.

## Target behavior evidence

- Renee's personal history, if disclosed, should be a bounded personal account, not an objective universal rule.
- That fact should meaningfully shape her later decision: attention to accountable handoffs, supervision, sign-out, responsibility, or limits.
- Malik should show a distinct position grounded in guided practice, not simply echo Renee or act as a generic contrarian.
- Tessa should contribute a practical system and adjust it when the actual staffing/continuity constraint becomes clear.
- The player proposal should receive a reasoned, proportionate response. NPCs may disagree, negotiate, or accept a limited trial.
- At the one-week return, NPCs should remember what actually happened, distinguish observed trial results from assumptions, and show proportionate learning. Renee can become more comfortable with a supervised process while retaining her preference for accountable handoffs.
- If the scene has not established a detail, preserve UNKNOWN rather than inventing it.

## Review dimensions (0–4; evidence required)

- NPC fact-to-decision causality
- Distinct NPC judgment and behavior
- Response to unplanned player proposal
- Proportionate character change over time
- Natural scene flow and handoff of player agency
- Information and knowledge boundaries

A 3 indicates good, playable performance. A 4 is exceptional. Insufficiently covered dimensions should be N/A / evidence-limited.

## Limits

This is one synthetic scene sequence in one assistant session. It cannot establish cross-session memory, real multi-turn platform behavior, independent review, or stable real-table performance.
