# Blades in the Dark｜PC / Crew Ability Runtime Matrix

> Primary source: user-provided Chinese core rulebook V1.3 (English original 2017 Digital Release v8.2).  
> Cross-check: official Full Player's Kit v8.2.  
> Purpose: a trigger-oriented operational index for abilities already selected on active PC/Crew sheets.  
> The descriptions below are original short summaries, not replacement ability text. Exact trigger wording and exceptions remain tied to the selected official sheet/version.

## 1. Runtime rules

1. Only abilities recorded as owned by the active PC/Crew can trigger. Unselected catalogue abilities are inert.
2. Check an ability at its event boundary: before a roll, when a consequence is assigned, when an activity is chosen, at Score/Session/Downtime closeout, or when a persistent fiction permission becomes relevant.
3. Apply each benefit once per trigger unless its text explicitly grants a count, duration, or repeated use. Record per-Score limits and spent resources.
4. Distinguish `+1d`, `+1 Effect`, `Potency`, `Quality`, `Scale`, `Position`, `result level`, and permission to attempt an action. They are not interchangeable.
5. Special Armor abilities are explicit resource options. Mark the Special Armor box when used; the ability's own text governs the eligible consequence/action and whether it avoids Stress.
6. Veteran abilities must record their source Playbook/Crew and exact version. A Veteran pick does not grant every ability from that source.
7. Crew-wide abilities affect only the scope stated in their text; do not automatically apply them to Cohorts, vehicles, or allied NPCs.
8. If an ability says `each PC`, apply to every current PC; if it says `your cohort`, check the relevant Cohort; if it says `you`, do not expand it to the whole Crew.

## 4. Ability interaction and adjudication rules

### 4.1 Action rating increases
- Crew abilities such as Deadly/Dangerous/Chosen/Silver Tongues/Renegades/Everyone Steals add +1 Action rating to a listed Action, with max 3. They do not grant +1d and do not increase the rating above 3. If Deadly is selected during initial character/Crew creation, its official text says it supersedes the normal starting Action rating limit.
- Apply the Crew ability when setting up PC ratings/advancement state, not as a temporary die bonus on every roll.
- If multiple sources affect the same rating, follow the full sheet's stacking/cap language; never exceed the stated max 3 unless another explicit rule (e.g. Mastery) permits it.

### 4.2 Special Armor abilities
- Trigger must match the named consequence or action category. A Special Armor box is not a universal Resistance roll.
- Mark the Special Armor use when expended. For abilities that let it replace Push Yourself, choose one listed normal Push benefit (+1d, +1 Effect, or ignore a severe Harm penalty); the ability waives the usual 2 Stress. This is an alternative way to Push, not a free extra benefit.
- For abilities that use Special Armor to resist an eligible consequence, resolve the ability's own wording: most listed abilities say the consequence is completely avoided, while Whisper's Warded says it cancels or reduces the severity. Do not generalize one ability's wording to another.
- Several abilities explicitly allow remote protection: Spider's Mastermind and Whisper's Warded state that the PC need not be present, provided the player narrates how prior preparation or protection applies. This is not blanket permission to resist any remote consequence.
- Special Armor restores at the beginning of Downtime, as stated in the relevant ability texts. Do not apply this timing to ordinary Armor / Heavy Armor: those recover when Load is selected for the next Score (see R-005 in RULE_ISSUES.md).

### 4.3 Scope, stacking, and timing
- +1d to a roll does not automatically add to a related Fortune Roll or Engagement Roll unless the ability names that roll.
- +1 Effect changes impact, not the Action Roll's highest die outcome.
- Potency addresses supernatural opposition/effectiveness; it is not a free +1d.
- Cohort Quality/Scale modifiers apply to Cohort actions; PC bonuses do not automatically transfer to the Cohort.
- Downtime abilities resolve at their named point. `At the end of Downtime` occurs after ordinary activities; `during Downtime` may be used within that phase; `per Score` counters reset for the next Score.
- Persistent permissions (e.g. Ghost Echoes, Ghost Passage, immunity) remain active while the ability is owned; record new facts or exceptions created in play.
