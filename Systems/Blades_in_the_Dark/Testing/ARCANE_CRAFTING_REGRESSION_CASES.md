# Blades in the Dark｜Arcane & Crafting Regression Cases

> Basis: user-provided Chinese core rulebook V1.3, PDF pp.228–231 (English original 2017 Digital Release v8.2).
> Type: source-grounded manual regression cases. These are expected-result checks, not an automated rules engine or live-play validation.
> Scope: Ritual, Magnitude, Invention, Crafting, Modification, Drawbacks.

## Ritual

| ID | Input / trigger | Expected handling | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARC-R01 | PC has Ritual, knows the ritual, and satisfies its recorded method and prerequisites; no danger and no uncertainty | Spend the ritual's established Downtime Activity cost and Stress; resolve the defined effect without inventing an unnecessary Action or Fortune Roll | pp.228–229 |
| ARC-R02 | Ritual performance is dangerous, and its effect is also uncertain | Make both rolls: Action Roll for danger/unplanned consequences; Fortune Roll for the uncertain effect. Do not collapse them into one roll | p.229 |
| ARC-R03 | A Downtime ritual Fortune Roll has been made; player spends 2 Coin | Raise the result level by 2, using the Downtime 1:1 Coin rule; this represents costly/rare materials | p.229 |
| ARC-R04 | Ritual is powerful or far-reaching | GM may require two or more Downtime Activities; a ritual partly completed in Downtime may be recorded as primed and released by its specified final incantation/action | p.229 |
| ARC-R05 | PC lacks the Ritual Special Ability but wants to study/practice ritual sorcery | Explain the stated danger: learning and practicing without the ability can expose the PC to harm from the supplicated powers. Do not present this as safe routine research. Performing a ritual requires the ability | PC ability text p.92; ritual text p.229 |

## Invention and Crafting

| ID | Input / trigger | Expected handling | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARC-C01 | PC invents a new special formula/design | Start a Long-Term Project, usually an 8-segment clock; answer all four invention questions: type/function, minimum Quality, obscurity, drawbacks | p.230 |
| ARC-C02 | PC gains Alchemist, Artificer, or Strange Methods | The relevant ability supplies one special starting formula/design without a separate invention project; it also improves matching invention/crafting result level as stated by the ability | pp.230–231; ability text |
| ARC-C03 | Crew Tier 2, no Workshop, Tinker roll is 4/5, no Coin or ability modifier | Base produced Quality is 2 | p.231 |
| ARC-C04 | Crew Tier 2 has Workshop; Tinker roll is 4/5; no Coin or ability modifier | Effective Tier for this crafting roll is 3, so produced Quality is 3 | p.230–231 |
| ARC-C05 | A crafted item needs minimum Quality 4, but final produced Quality is 3 | Do not silently grant the full intended effect. State the shortfall and determine a lesser result or further work supported by the fiction | p.231 |
| ARC-C06 | Modify an existing item at Crew Tier 2 | Required Quality thresholds are 3 for a simple useful feature, 4 for a significant modification, and 5 for an arcane/spark-craft/alchemical modification | p.231 |
| ARC-C07 | An alchemical creation is designed without the Consumable drawback | Invalid design: all alchemical compounds must have Consumable, usually one use | p.231 |
| ARC-C08 | A Conspicuous creation is used multiple times during one Score | Apply +1 Heat once for that Score/operation, not once per individual use | p.231 |
