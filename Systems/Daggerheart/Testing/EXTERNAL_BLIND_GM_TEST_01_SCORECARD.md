# External Blind GM Test 01 — Evaluator Scorecard

<badge>Evaluator Only — Keep out of GM context</badge>

Score each item from 0 to 2:

- **0 — Fail:** materially incorrect, absent, or harmful to play.
- **1 — Partial:** broadly workable but inconsistent, unclear, or overexplained.
- **2 — Pass:** correct, clear, and integrated naturally into play.

Do not score encounter balance or solo difficulty in this test.

## Critical failure conditions

Any of the following is an automatic fail regardless of total score:

- Treating Success with Fear as a failure.
- Charging another Fear for the automatic Undertow triggered by Failure with Fear.
- Double-charging the generic extra-Spotlight cost when Patient Hunter itself grants the Glass Snake its Spotlight.
- Treating a Reaction Roll as an Action Roll that generates Hope or Fear.
- Revealing the courier's location or the bandit's identity before the player has a fair way to discover it.
- Forcing a single crossing method after the player proposes a credible alternative.
