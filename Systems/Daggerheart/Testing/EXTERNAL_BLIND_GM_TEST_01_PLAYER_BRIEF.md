# External Blind GM Test 01 — Player Brief

> Test scene only. This is not a campaign or a canonical module.  
> Use existing Tier 1 character(s); do not spend time on character creation. Core Set only.

## The crossing

A storm has washed away the old footbridge at Greywater Ford. A courier named Tamsin left before dawn carrying a sealed dispatch for the hill watch, but she has not arrived. The dispatch contains a warning that must reach the watch post before dusk.

The village's ferryman, Halen, says the river is too fast to cross casually. His rope-guided punt is still here, but the guide rope has been cut. A child named Jory claims to have seen a flash from somewhere in the current. Across the water, a narrow bluff rises above the far bank.

You are on the near bank. The river is loud enough that conversation is difficult. Reeds and broken branches rush past. There is no bridge.

Halen is willing to talk, but he is worried about the boat and reluctant to send anyone into the current. Jory is curious and keeps glancing at the water.

## What you want

Find Tamsin, recover the sealed dispatch, and get it to the hill watch before dusk.

You can investigate, question people, repair or replace the rope, use equipment or abilities, attempt a crossing, seek another route, or do something unexpected. There is no required solution.

## Test boundaries

- Bring your current Tier 1 PC(s); use the character sheet and equipment you already have.
- This is a short scene, not a full campaign.
- You may ask the GM questions, propose plans, and take actions freely.
- Do not read the GM Oracle or Evaluator Scorecard before the test.
